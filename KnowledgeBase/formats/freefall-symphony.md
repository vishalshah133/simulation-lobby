# Format: Freefall Symphony (`fall`)

**Status:** `building` (2026-10-20) · **First video:** [fall-001](../../plans/videos/fall-001-freefall-symphony.md) (long form) · Short cut `fall-002`

## The idea

A marble falls down an endless vertical well, and **every bounce plays the next note of a song**. The pads aren't placed
first and hoped for. They're solved from the song: each pad sits exactly where the marble will be when its note is due. So the
rhythm is the composer's, and the motion is still real ballistics (a frictionless impulse on each pad). The pads stay lit, so each
song leaves a glowing sculpture behind it.

It's hypnotic, sound-on spectacle, a new genre for the channel. Inspired by MIKAN 3D's *Abstract World* (14.5M, a journey through
abstract worlds) and the music-marble channels (song recognition made the genre's 2023-24 mega-hits), but it copies neither: it's a
vertical fall instead of a ride, the marble plays the world, and pitch is visible (distance from the axis plus hue).

## Hooks that should carry it

- **Recognition.** Each world's song becomes recognisable within ~4 s. That's the payoff, repeated once per act.
- **Anticipation.** Each pad fades in just before contact, so the eye learns the rhythm and predicts the next hit.
- **Reveal.** At a world's end, the camera pulls back to show the song's sculpture, and the title appears.
- **A twist with a musical reason.** In *Canon in D* the marble splits into voices that enter in rounds, because a canon *is* rounds.
- **Climax.** *Mountain King*'s accelerando tightens the arcs and spins the vortex up into a white core.

## Rules learned (fill in after the first render)

- Tuning lives in the per-world table in the config (bpm, passes, gravity). The kernel stays the same.
- Melodies must be exact. A wrong note in a recognition video is like a wrong answer in a maths Short.
