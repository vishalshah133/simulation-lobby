## Premise

Four colours race down a volcano. Every ×2 gate doubles your marbles, the lava swallows whatever it catches, and the
smallest colour after each stage is out. **Which colour survives the lava?**

Why this video: it's the next long-form format decided on 2026-10-16 (`channel-strategy.md` → *10-16, from MIKAN*). It's
MIKAN's best-performing shape (survival + proliferation) with our signature mechanic (multiply gates) on top, at the
decided 10-12 min length, and it's meant to be the first episode of a numbered series (*Lava Run #1*).

## The plot

The whole video is one place: **a volcano with four stages of track stacked down its side**, a lava lake at the
bottom and a round island in the middle of the lake. It's visible in full in the first frame, so the viewer sees
the whole journey (and the finale) before anything moves.

1. **The setup (0:00-0:25).** A 20 s flyover of the whole course, then four colour pens at the crater rim, 5 marbles each. The crater glows and rumbles. The
   question and three rules come up, then a countdown. Nothing moves yet: this is the anticipation beat.
2. **Stage 1, THE SLOPES (0:25-2:45).** The gates drop and the marbles pour onto wide, gentle switchbacks. ×2 gates
   arrive every few seconds, and the counts explode from 5 to 30-60 per colour. Eight seconds in, **the crater
   erupts** and a lava front starts flowing down the track behind them, slowly. The early deaths are stragglers stuck
   in peg gardens. A fork offers a **×3 lane guarded by a pinwheel** against a safe ×1 lane, so the colours split.
   The stage ends at a basin with a door. The lava closes in and the last stragglers race for it. The door shuts,
   counts freeze, and the smallest colour is out: **"YELLOW IS OUT."**
3. **Drop (2:40-3:00).** The basin floor opens and the survivors pour into Stage 2's pens. The counter bar recaps.
4. **Stage 2, THE SWITCHBACKS (3:00-5:20).** Tighter hairpins, a bottleneck, two pinwheels. The lava is faster
   ("LAVA SPEED UP"). There are more ×2 gates, but each clone appears *behind* its parent, closer to the lava, so
   multiplying is only worth it if you're far enough ahead. A colour that multiplied greedily loses its tail. Second
   elimination at the door: **"BLUE IS OUT."** Two colours left.
5. **Midpoint twist, Stage 3: THE BRIDGE (5:30-8:00).** One last **×5 gate**, right before a long bridge over the lava
   lake that's only two marbles wide, with no rails. The bigger colour pours hundreds of marbles into the jam. They
   push each other off the edge into the lava, and the jam is still on the bridge when the lava front arrives. The
   colour that was winning on numbers loses most of them. This is the comeback: **a big crowd is a weakness on the
   bridge.** No elimination this stage. Whoever is left crosses to the island.
6. **Finale, Stage 4: THE ISLAND (8:15-10:30).** The survivors of both colours drop onto a round platform in the middle
   of the lava lake. There are no gates any more, just survival. **The outer ring cracks and sinks, one ring at a
   time**, and a **sweeper arm** turns across the island, slowly at first and then faster. Marbles shoved off the edge
   hiss into the lava. When fewer than six remain, everything slows down so the last duel gets room.
7. **Payoff (10:30-10:50).** The last marble of the losing colour goes in. The camera pushes in on the survivors,
   confetti fires in their colour, and the caption reads **"RED SURVIVES THE LAVA!"** End card: "Did your colour make
   it? 👇"

### Scene

`Assets/Scenes/Survival_LavaRun_3D.unity`. Menu: **Simulation Lobby ▸ 3D Formats ▸ Survival (surv) ▸ Lava Run (surv-002)**
(priority 2xx, next after Wall vs Ball).

- **Volcano:** a big low-poly cone (generated mesh, basalt-dark with an emissive crack texture), crater at the top with
  an emissive lava pool. Four track tiers wrap down its front face, so all four are in frame from the hero camera.
- **Tracks:** four `TrackSection` layouts (`LavaRun_Stage1..3` + the island), built with the promoted
  `TrackMeshBuilder`/`ObstacleBuilder`. Each stage ends in a **basin** with a door (kinematic slab) and a trapdoor
  floor that drops into the next stage's pen.
- **Lava lake + island:** a flat emissive plane with scrolling noise at the base. The island is a round platform made
  of **concentric ring segments** (4-5 rings), each its own kinematic collider so it can sink.
- **Lava front:** a swept mesh along the stage's `TrackPath`, from the top to the current front distance, slightly above
  the track floor, emissive orange with a bright leading lip. Smoke plume (`SmokePlumeFx`) and dust at the lip.

### Simulation / rules

Determinism contract: everything below happens in `Tick(fixedDelta)`, from the seeded `System.Random`. PhysX steps on
the fixed tick, as in `terr`.

- **Teams:** 4 colours, `startMarblesPerTeam` (5) each, placed in pens with seeded jitter.
- **Stage loop (Stages 1-3):** `Pen → gate opens → eruptionDelay → lava front advances → door closes when the front reaches
  the basin → stage result`. The lava front is a single distance `s_lava` along the stage's `TrackPath`, advancing at
  `lavaSpeed[stage]` m/s (ramping within the stage via `EscalationRule`).
- **Death by lava:** each tick, each live marble's progress `s` (via `TrackPath.ProgressAt`, hinted from last tick) is
  compared with `s_lava`. `s < s_lava + lavaKillMargin` → killed, returned to the pool. Event `Swallowed(team, position)`.
- **Death by falling:** below a kill plane, or touching the lake/lava collider → `FellIn(team, position)`. This is the
  main killer on the bridge and the island.
- **×N gates:** `TriggerZone3D` across the track. On a marble's **first** entry (one trigger per marble per gate, tracked
  by pool index), spawn N−1 clones at the gate's entry plane, **behind** the parent (`cloneBackOffset`), with the
  parent's velocity plus a small seeded lateral jitter. Clones inherit the team and are fresh to every later gate.
  There is no cap by default (`maxLivePerTeam` = 0); pools grow on demand. Set it above 0 for a hard cap, where extra
  clones aren't spawned (event `GateCapped`, which only fires with a cap set).
- **Stage end:** the door closes when `s_lava` reaches the basin entry. Marbles inside the basin are safe and the rest
  die. If two or more colours are alive, the **smallest** is eliminated: its basin marbles are removed (sink with
  embers). Ties go to the colour whose last marble reached the basin **latest**. That's deterministic and on screen.
  Stage 3 (the bridge) does **not** eliminate.
- **Wipe-out:** a colour at 0 live marbles is out immediately (`TeamOut`). If only one colour is left at any point, it
  wins and the run skips to the payoff.
- **Island (Stage 4):** survivors drop onto the island. Every `ringSinkInterval` seconds the outermost remaining ring
  sinks (a 1.5 s tremble, then it lowers into the lava). The sweeper (`KinematicSpinner`) speeds up per
  `EscalationRule`. When total alive drops below `slowdownBelow` (6), sink interval and sweeper speed scale by
  `finaleSlowdown`. The innermost disc never sinks. Ends when one colour is left, with a hard cap `maxRunSeconds` (forced
  decision: the colour with the most marbles wins, event `ForcedDecision`).
- **Completion gate:** `IsComplete` only after the payoff hold. The pens start at rest, so there is no settle-based completion.
- **Result metrics:** per-stage alive counts, eliminations, deaths by cause, gate triggers, peak live bodies,
  `GateCapped` count, lead changes (colour with the most marbles), run length.

**Targets per run** (read from result metrics across ~10 Plays, then tune; this isn't seed hunting):
- Run length **10:00-12:00**.
- Peak live per colour 40-120, and no cap hit (cap is off by default).
- Stage 1 and 2 each eliminate exactly one colour, and no colour is wiped out before Stage 1 ends.
- **Lead changes ≥ 2**, including at least one at the bridge or island (the twist must be able to flip it).
- The island lasts 90-150 s.

### Look

Elegant-materials rule (memory): glossy basalt with emissive orange cracks, glass-lacquer marbles in four high-contrast
hues (**RED, BLUE, GREEN, YELLOW**, chosen to read against orange lava: blue and green get the most contrast; red gets
a white rim so it doesn't vanish into the lava), chrome gate arches with a coloured flash, lava with an HDR emissive
lip and bloom. Night sky, a warm key from the lava below, a cool rim from above, drifting embers (`AmbientDriftField`).

- **HUD** (uGUI + TMP, 1080x1920 reference, match height): top question, the 4-colour **counter bar** under it, a bottom
  caption for beats and a footer for the stage. **`ShareBarHud` needs a count-label mode** (`RED 34` instead of
  `RED 26%`): a small format-agnostic addition in `Presentation`, pushed by a thin binder in the format.
- **Gate decals** (`×2`, `×3`, `×5`) are world-space TMP on the arches (an allowed arena decal).
- **Camera:** a `FocusCameraRig` home pose per stage, framing *the gap between the lava front and the pack*, which is
  where the tension is. It focuses for gate bursts, the door slam, the bridge jam and island ring sinks, plus a wide
  between stages. The eruption gets a shake.
- **Audio** (procedural, `ProceduralToneBank`): a lava rumble whose loudness tracks the front's distance to the
  nearest marble, a gate chime that rises in pitch with the team's count, a hiss + thud on each death (rate-limited),
  a door slam, ring-crack, and a winner chord.

## Build notes (2026-10-16)

Changes from the plan made during build review:
- **Lava chases the pack**: front speed = max(ramped speed, catch-up toward the rearmost marble minus a gap that
  shrinks per stage, `lavaMaxGapStart/End`). A fixed speed would have let the pack outrun it.
- **Bridge 65 m**, not open-ended: a long railless single file kills by attrition before any jam.
- **Survivors are re-seated** into the next pens (pens sit under the trapdoor), the door closes early once every live
  marble is in the basin, and eliminated marbles are removed instantly with presenter ghosts sinking in their place.
- **Camera**: marbles are 0.22 m on a ~224 m volcano, so the wide shot can't carry the story. The hook opens close on the
  pens, stages use a 5-12 m chase on the tail + lava lip, and the island pushes in as marbles die.
- **Tuning lever debt**: marble `linearDamping` is a builder constant (`MarbleLinearDamping`), not a config field. Move it
  into `LavaRunConfig` if it needs changing between videos.
- Estimated stages ~145 / ~160 / ~90 s, so ~10 min with the island. Real Play numbers decide.
